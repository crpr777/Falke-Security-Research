**Falke Security Research** is a small public notebook for technical write-ups. Posts are grouped into three topics:

- **Covert Methods of Entry** — physical and technical entry tradecraft, detection, and defensive implications
- **Red Team Operations** — adversary simulation, planning, and after-action notes
- **AI Security Research** — attacks, defenses, and evaluation of AI-assisted systems

This site is a foundation, not a finished magazine. New pieces are Markdown files in `_posts/`. There is no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Replace this page with a short bio, contact method, or disclosure policy when you are ready.
